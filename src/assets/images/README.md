# Landing-page concept imagery

`kifaru-workspace.webp` and `kifaru-models.webp` were extracted from the embedded
WebP assets in the user-supplied `bastco_kifaru_visual_pricing.html` design concept.
They are decorative workspace illustrations, not photographs of Bastco's office,
customer environments, or screenshots of Kifaru. Both are rendered with empty alt
text; nearby text communicates the product information.

`kifaru-field-plate.svg` is an original rhino illustration from the former homepage hero.
It is a brand illustration, not an image of the product interface.

The site processes these local sources into responsive images with Astro. It does
not load images, fonts, or trackers from the original concept or third-party hosts.
Confirm publishing rights for the supplied artwork before public release.
